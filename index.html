FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which platforms can I import from?

Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube (including Shorts). You can also paste a caption or any workout text and ReelRack will structure it.

What if the workout is only shown as on-screen text?

That's exactly what ReelRack is built for. The AI watches the actual video — overlays, burned-in captions, demonstrated movements — so routines that are never spoken aloud still import accurately.

Is ReelRack free?

You can try ReelRack free. ReelRack Pro unlocks unlimited importing, scheduling, and progress tracking — with a 7-day free trial, cancel anytime.

Do you access my social media account?

No. ReelRack only processes the public post you explicitly share or paste. Your workouts, logs, and streaks live on your device.
