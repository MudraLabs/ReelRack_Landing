Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This policy explains what ReelRack (Mudra Labs, LLC) collects and how it's used.

1. What we collect
• Account: your Apple sign-in identifier and email (if shared) — used to authenticate you.
• Content you submit: links or captions you import. These are sent to our server to be converted into a workout.
• Usage counters: how many imports you've run (for fair-use limits).
• Purchases: subscription status is processed by Apple and RevenueCat. We never see your payment details.

2. What stays on your device
Your workouts, catalogs, schedules, completed sessions, set logs, streaks, and reel cover images are stored locally on your device. We do not run analytics trackers or ad SDKs.

3. AI processing
To convert a post into a workout, the link or caption you submit — and, for video links, the publicly available video — is processed by service providers: Apify (retrieves the public post), Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude (extract the routine), and Supadata (audio transcripts). These providers process the content to provide the feature and are not permitted to use it to identify you.

4. What we don't do
We don't sell your data. We don't access your social media accounts — only the public post you explicitly submit.

5. Deleting your data
More → Delete Account permanently removes your server-side account and usage records. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data.

6. Children
ReelRack is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes
We'll post any changes here and note material changes in the app.
