Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

ReelRack ("we," "our," or "us") is made by Mudra Labs, LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the ReelRack app and related services (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to this policy.

1. Information you provide
• Account information: when you sign in with Apple, we receive your Apple sign-in identifier and, if you choose to share it, your email address. Used solely to authenticate you.
• Workout content: when you import a workout (an Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube link, or pasted text), we process the URL, caption, and the publicly available video to generate a structured routine.
• Communications: if you contact support, we receive the information in your message.
• Payments: subscriptions are processed by Apple. Subscription status is managed by RevenueCat. We never see or store your payment details.

2. Information collected automatically
• Usage counters: how many imports you've run, used to enforce fair-use limits.
• Server logs: our import service keeps short-lived technical logs (such as request status and errors) to keep the Service reliable.
We do not run analytics trackers or advertising SDKs, and we do not collect your location.

3. What stays on your device
Your workouts, catalogs, schedules, completed sessions, set logs, streaks, and reel cover images are stored locally on your device — not on our servers.

4. How we use information
• Provide and improve the Service
• Convert imported links into structured routines
• Enforce fair-use limits and prevent abuse
• Process subscriptions
• Respond to support requests

5. Workout content and AI processing
• Temporary processing: links, captions, and video content are processed transiently to extract the workout, then discarded; original videos are not permanently stored on our servers.
• AI providers: content you submit is processed by service providers acting on our behalf — a content-retrieval service (to fetch the public post you submit) and AI model providers such as Google and Anthropic (to extract the routine). These providers process the content solely to provide the feature, are not permitted to use it to identify you, and do not use it to train their models.
• No training: your content is not used to train AI models.
• No facial recognition: we do not perform facial recognition, biometric identification, or face analysis.
• Private to you: imported workouts are visible only to you and are never shared publicly.

6. Social media links
We only process the publicly available content of links you explicitly submit. We cannot process private accounts, and we never ask for or store your social media credentials.

7. Sharing and disclosure
We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We share information only with the service providers above (to operate the Service), if required by law, in connection with a business transfer, or with your consent.

8. Third-party services
Apple (sign-in, payments, distribution), Supabase (authentication and backend), RevenueCat (subscriptions), and content-retrieval and AI model providers such as Google and Anthropic (workout extraction). Each operates under its own privacy policy.

9. Security
We use appropriate technical and organizational measures, including encryption in transit. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Retention and deletion
We keep personal information only as long as needed to provide the Service. More → Delete Account permanently removes your server-side account and usage records. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data.

11. Your rights
Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, and to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise them.

12. Children
ReelRack is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If such data is discovered, we will delete it promptly.

13. International transfers
Your information is processed in the United States and may be processed in other countries by the providers listed above, with appropriate safeguards.

14. Changes
We may update this policy and will post changes here with a new "Last updated" date, noting material changes in the app.
